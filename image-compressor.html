How it works

  1. Choose one image from your device.

Formats and limits

JPEG, PNG, and WebP images up to 20 MB are supported. Animated images, SVG, PDF, video, and batch processing are outside the MVP.

FAQ

Will my image be uploaded?

No. Image bytes stay in your browser for local processing.

Can a compressed image be larger?

Yes. Some already-optimized files can become larger after re-encoding, so the result will be reported honestly.

Does PNG quality work like JPEG quality?

Not usually. PNG is lossless in the browser canvas pipeline, so WebP may be the better option when smaller output matters.
